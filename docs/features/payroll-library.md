# Payroll library and name matching

### The wider payroll library (`src/lib/payroll/`, ~30 modules, most load-bearing ones have a paired `.test.ts`)

- **`domain.ts`** — shared kernel: Satpam post/pay-type constants and resolvers, the dated Satpam rules (`satpamRatesForDutyDate`/`satpamRatesForPeriod`, `satpamShiftTimes`, `satpamPayTypeIssue`: from duty date `SATPAM_POLICY_V2_START_DATE` = 2026-10-01, Harian Rp15.000, 8-hour shifts 07–15/15–23/23–07 (each shift ends at the next one's start, so the 30 Sep night runs 22–07), no Lembur Sendiri; never read an undated table), payroll-period-token helpers, `calculatePayrollTotals`, and the status guards `isImmutablePayrollStatus`/`isTransferEligibleStatus` that gate whether a slip can still be edited. Most other payroll modules import from here.
- **`driverJourney.ts`** — SOPIR trip wages: per-vehicle rate table, distance/duration/night-premium (`calculateNightPremium`) constants, meal allowance tiers, fuel procurement modes (hold_accumulate/procure_release/standard_direct), reimbursement settlement (`calculateDriverReimbursementSettlement`), net wage (`calculateDriverNetWage`), and journey timeline normalization (`calculateEditableDriverJourneyTimeline` — the single source of truth for overnight/lintas-hari inference, shared by submission and audit). **Authorization since docs/adr/0001** (`authorizationRule: 'measured_loop'`): the Kepala SatKer measures the closed loop and plans departure (on `activityDate`) and return; Jatah BBM = loop × rate (`calculateDriverJourneyLoopOperationalCosts`) and the estimate is the exact `calculatePlannedDriverJourney` Upah Bersih (no 1.25× range; `authorizedDriverWageEstimate` shows older journeys' range). Such a journey stores `totalDistanceKm`/`plannedRouteDurationHours` (the loop), half the loop in `distanceKm`/`durationHours` for readers that double them, and the planned Durasi perjalanan in `customDurationPP`; the sopir report seeds its Komponen Waktu from `plannedRouteDurationHours`. The API refuses the old one-way body with 409.
- **`driverJourneyOverlap.ts`** — one sopir cannot drive two journeys at once. `driverJourneySpan` turns a report's dates/times into a Jakarta-time span (same timeline rule as pay), `findOverlappingDriverReport` compares it with the sopir's other `SOPIR` reports (pending or approved; declined ones block nothing; touching at a boundary is fine). Enforced in the same transaction by `src/lib/server/driverJourneyOverlap.ts` on the sopir's submit (`/api/pekarya/activities`, 409) and on the auditor's `approve_driver` (`/api/pekarya/activities/review`, 409) — in the audit only for a first approval or a changed timeline, so an old overlap does not block an unrelated re-audit. Claiming a journey is not checked.
- **`vehicleFuel.ts`** — Firestore-backed vehicle fuel ledger (reservation state machine: none→reserved→committed→released) supporting `driverJourney.ts`'s procurement modes.
- **`driverPiket.ts`** — driver standby ("piket") duty scheduling across 5 fixed stations + ad-hoc extras. Also the sopir's vehicle switch (`driverVehicleChangeBlocker` / `planDriverVehicleChange`, action `change_vehicle` on `/api/driver-journeys`, opened from the vehicle pill on the journey report): only on a self-authorized journey still `claimed` with no BBM held, since nothing is priced or held there until submission. To Ndalem forces Standard; from Ndalem the sopir picks the mode; car to car keeps it. Each switch is appended to the journey's `driverVehicleChanges`, copied onto the ActivityReport at submission and shown in the audit dialog. Kepala-Satker-authorized journeys stay correctable only in the audit.
- **`satpamDutyPlan.ts`** — generates/validates the 8-day Satpam rotation plan, handles Libur swaps, classifies duty assignments into pay types (Harian/Jumat & Libur/Lembur Sendiri/Lembur Cover/Off-Duty).
- **`satpamAttendance.ts`** — default scan-in/out times per Satpam shift + scan-range validation.
- **`pekaryaSpj.ts`** — Pekarya job categories (SATPAM/SOPIR/PEKARYA/TEKNISI/KEBERSIHAN/KEBERSIHAN_PONTI/PONTI) and activity types; computes approved SPJ amounts and payroll period windows.
- **`pekaryaOfficialLeave.ts`** — official-leave (`izin_resmi`) fixed scan times and request lifecycle.
- **`attendance.ts`** — general Pekarya attendance normalization/consolidation (NIPY/PIN resolution, duplicate-day consolidation) and time-based pay. Kebersihan and Teknisi use `work_window_inferred`: interpret imported scans at/before 07:30 as arrivals and at/after 14:00 as departures when placed on the wrong side; interpret each duplicate row before choosing the earliest arrival/latest departure, excluding separate overnight pairs from daytime attendance. Fill a missing side at 07:30/14:00. Valid pairs within the window, manual corrections, absence decisions and raw evidence remain intact; night-only pairs are left for review. Other categories and Loyalis retain the 150-minute default. Publications store `singleScanPolicy`; closed periods retain their stored policy (including the earlier `work_window` rule). An open publication on an outdated policy is rewritten by the automatic sync (`pekaryaAttendanceSync.ts`); until then saving payroll or closing the period is refused.
- **`loyalisPresenceWindow.ts`** / **`loyalisPresenceWorkbook.ts`** — Loyalis presence: single-scan auto-fill and work-window (07:30–14:00) duration calculation; parsing of uploaded presence Excel workbooks into normalized rows with issue flagging.
- **`gantiLibur.ts`** — Loyalis-only ganti libur (compensatory day off): working a full 07:30–14:00 on a Jumat/Tanggal Merah earns one working day off (less is lembur, which SAKU does not pay — the request is declined); max 2 days off per Saturday–Friday week (the week ends on the Loyalis weekly day off); the worked holiday and the day off may come in either order. Employees submit from `/employee/leave`; Admin Karyawan/Super Admin approve on `/dashboard/payroll/uraian/presence-corrections` only once the worked date's `LoyalisPresence` shows the full window. An approved day off is overlaid onto presence as a `GANTI LIBUR` row by the same `applyApprovedPaidLeaveToLoyalisEntry` that writes `CUTI` rows. A request may carry up to 5 optional surat resmi files (`gantiLiburAttachments.ts`): uploaded through `/api/uploads/ganti-libur` into Storage `ganti_libur/{employeeId}/`, then named by path on submit — the server re-reads each object's metadata (`loadGantiLiburAttachments`) instead of trusting a client-sent URL.
- **`loyalisAutoLeave.ts`** — automatic annual cuti for a Loyalis absent (`TIDAK HADIR`) on a working day (not Jumat/Tanggal Merah) with no leave submission: each such day spends one day of the balance and counts as `CUTI`; with no balance left (or before the first entitlement, each day held to its own date's tier) it stays an absence. Only fully absent days, earliest first; a day with any pending/approved cuti, ganti libur or presence correction is left alone. It applies from period `LOYALIS_AUTO_LEAVE_START_PERIOD` (2026-09) and never touches closed periods or final slips. `POST /api/attendance/loyalis/auto-leave` (`src/lib/server/loyalisAutoLeave.ts`) runs on manual saves and autosaves. Payroll refreshes through `/api/payroll/loyalis-presence` and `/api/payroll/uraian-propagation` also reconcile the allowance before calculating pay. Each run re-derives the month from saved raw presence and writes approved `AnnualPaidLeaveRequests` marked `source: 'auto_absence'` (plus the payroll post, balance `usedDays`, revisions and audit records) or releases days that no longer qualify (request → `withdrawn`, post deleted, `usedDays` back). The payroll dashboard and attendance table show effective `CUTI` scans at 07:30–14:00 and a full daily credit; raw absence rows remain available so a correction can release the day. Refreshes are idempotent. Test: `npm run test:loyalis-auto-leave:integration`.
- **`pekaryaAutoLeave.ts`** — automatic annual cuti for all seven Pekarya categories from September 2026. `POST /api/attendance/pekarya/auto-leave` accepts `period` and `category` (`ALL` supported); Super Admin and Finance may reconcile, while Kepala SatKer is restricted to permitted categories. Regular working-day full absences use 07:30–14:00. Satpam uses one published scheduled duty and its exact dated shift bounds, paid as Harian or Jumat & Libur at `satpamRatesForDutyDate`; an approved work report never spends cuti. No matching employee/date rows, duplicate identities, pending submissions, future duties, stale schedules, or incomplete overnight evidence are held for review. Next-month evidence is required for a month-end night. Imported evidence is preserved; automatic regular credits overlay the shared effective attendance, and Satpam credits enter existing annual-leave reconciliation. Requests/posts carry `source: auto_absence`; one transaction updates the annual balance, revisions, audit, and a dirty/version record in backend-only `PekaryaAutoLeaveStates`. Actual corrections and shift approvals refund only automatic leave atomically. Attendance totals/bonuses already in the rekap are recomputed; the automatic attendance sync, draft propagation/save, closure and locking reject dirty or changed versions. The rule runs after import/source commands and before staff refreshes; employee/attendance GETs do not apply cuti. Closed periods and final slips are preserved. Tests: `npm run test:pekarya-auto-leave:integration`. Read-only deployment preview: `npm run preview:pekarya-auto-leave -- --period YYYY-MM`.

- **`presenceCorrections.ts`** — employee-submitted presence correction requests (tap_in/tap_out/both/izin_resmi) and their pending/approved/rejected lifecycle.
- **`calendar.ts`** — per-period work calendar and premium-date (Friday/holiday) resolution.
- **`koperasiLoan.ts`** — read-only loan status/installment/balance projection at a given period, restructuring lineage.
- **`koperasiLoanApplication.ts`** — write-side application rules: min/max amount & tenor, admin fees, eligibility, restructuring quotes.
- **`koperasiAmounts.ts`** / **`koperasiNames.ts`** — builds per-employee deduction/savings maps for payslips, matched by normalized name.
- **`proposalExpenseReports.ts`** / **`proposalExpenseApproval.ts`** — LPJ expense-report CRUD, budget-vs-actual totals, approval validation.
- **`slipBuilders.ts`** — canonical earnings/deductions row builders for a payslip (Firebase-free, reachable from API routes).
- **`payrollTax.ts`** — income tax as a slip category of its own (never a deduction row): a slip whose pre-tax Gaji Bersih reaches Rp 6.000.000 is charged a flat 5% of that same figure. Selection is manual (a super admin toggles it in Tinjau Slip Gaji) and is persisted as `taxApplied`; the *amount* is always derived server-side from the rows being written, so every propagation re-derives it.
- **`slipPropagation.ts`** — rules for pushing an employee-profile edit onto an already-saved slip (which fields are profile-owned vs. owned by koperasi/presence/SPJ systems).
- **`loyalisPresencePay.ts`** — how Loyalis attendance becomes Presensi and Bonus Presensi: `loyalisPresenceAmounts` (slip amounts, net from period 2026-11 and legacy full-then-deduct before it), `loyalisNetPresence` (one employee), `summarizeLoyalisPresenceDelta` (roster total vs. ideal attendance, shown on Presensi Loyalis) and `loyalisPresenceBreakdown` (reads either slip shape back for the payslip page and PDF).
- **`uraianPropagation.ts`** — counterpart for pushing Uraian rekap / Loyalis presence data onto draft slips; decides which slip rows a presence run owns. The writes themselves live in `src/lib/server/uraianSlipPropagation.ts` (`propagateUraianToDraftSlips`), shared by `POST /api/payroll/uraian-propagation` and the Pekarya attendance sync. It skips entries carrying `attendanceHold`, and a Pekarya run stops (409) if the rekap changes mid-run, since whoever changed it propagates again. The Loyalis raw presence page calls it on every autosave as well as on Simpan Data Presensi, so Loyalis pay follows each edit; pending correction requests and Bonus Triwulan stay behind the explicit save.
- **`pekaryaAttendanceSync.ts`** — Pekarya attendance reaches the Uraian rekap and the draft slips with no Publish step. The pure rules (fingerprint of what a category writes, `buildAutoPublishedEntries`, `attendanceHold`) are here; `src/lib/server/pekaryaAttendanceSync.ts` (`syncPekaryaAttendance`) runs them. It runs after every command that changes what a worker is owed: import, scan correction, manual NIPY link/unlink, izin/scan report review, cuti review, ganti libur review, calendar change, driver-journey review. It also runs from `POST /api/attendance/pekarya/auto-leave`, which Presensi Pekarya calls on every load, so a NIPY fixed on Data Pegawai is picked up too. Per category it rebuilds the view, writes `UraianGaji` + `PekaryaAttendancePublications` (`publishMode: 'automatic'`, `sourceFingerprint`), then propagates to draft slips. A command that only patched its own employees' entries updates just those slips. The write refuses if the publication document changed since the view was built (`AttendanceSyncConflict`, retried up to 3 times), so an older run never overwrites a newer one. Warnings no longer need acknowledging. An employee without a unique NIPY is held alone: their entry keeps its money and gets `attendanceHold`, slip creation and the slip preview refuse them, and propagation skips them until the NIPY is fixed. Satpam, closed periods and locked/paid slips are untouched (drift notices as usual). Outcomes go to server-only `PekaryaAttendanceSyncStates`; a failure never fails the command and shows on Presensi Pekarya with **Sinkronkan Ulang** (`POST /api/attendance/pekarya/sync`, Kepala SatKer/Super Admin). Test: `npm run test:pekarya-attendance:integration`.
- **`dashboardSlipData.ts`** — assembles the dashboard's period-level slip view across all the above. Closed periods retain saved rows, including legacy drafts, instead of refreshing their family allowance from today's profile.
- **`dashboardRoster.ts`** — the dashboard's period membership: closed months use `PayrollPeriods.payrollRosterSeal.employeeIds`, falling back to saved draft/final slips for legacy closed months without a seal. Open months use `buildPayrollRoster` plus any finalized slips. Current inactivity never removes a historical recipient; later hires are not backfilled into closed months. The cards, trend, breakdowns and drilldowns share this membership. `Pegawai Terhitung` includes drafts/previews; confirmation progress counts only immutable slips. Missing sealed slips block the summary instead of rebuilding historical salaries.
- **`payrollRoster.ts`** — builds the eligible-employee roster per payroll run.
- **`nipy.ts`** — generates/validates Pekarya NIPY employee-ID codes (category-prefixed: KEBERSIHAN=13, SOPIR=14, SATPAM=15, TEKNISI=16).
- **`vakasiTambahan.ts`** — guards against LPJ/proposal sandbox data leaking into real payroll earnings; also the counterpart for pushing an approved VakasiTambahan event onto draft slips (`POST /api/payroll/vakasi-propagation`), one earnings row per event name.
- **`roles.ts`** — `UserRole` union and capability checks (see `docs/rbac.md`).
- **`client.ts`** — browser-side authenticated fetch helpers for payroll API routes.

## Name Matching & Overrides

Since names in raw spreadsheet imports often mismatch official Firestore user documents:
- **`normalizeName(fullName)`**: strips trailing academic degrees, prepended titles (KH, Hj, Ust, Prof, etc.), lowercases/trims.
- **`MANUAL_OVERRIDES`**: hardcoded exact-match dictionary for edge cases (e.g. `Siti Rofiah` → `Siti Rofi'ah, A. Md.`).
- **`src/lib/payroll/koperasiNames.ts`**: equivalent name-normalization used specifically to match Koperasi loan/member records to payroll employees.
