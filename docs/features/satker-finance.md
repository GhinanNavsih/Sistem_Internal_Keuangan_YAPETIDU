# SatKer Finance

Double-entry books per unit at `/dashboard/satker-finance`, with server-owned writes through `/api/satker-finance`. Units live in `SatkerFinancialUnits` with `accountCatalog` and `fixedAssets`; academic-year books live in `SatkerFinancialYears/{unitId}_{academicYear}` with `entries`, `bankReconciliations`, and `audit`. Fiscal years run September–August.

Heads and assigned finance editors can record and correct transactions in any month or academic year. The app has no monthly submission, draft, approval, revision-request, or period-closing workflow. BAK receives the printed Arus Kas report at its office. Historical report, report-revision, notification, and closing records are retained in Firestore as inactive history; they are neither read by the app nor used to restrict edits. The former signed-report PDF and public QR-verification routes are removed.

- **Accounting integrity**: journals must balance. Used account identities remain stable, edits use optimistic revisions, deletions require a reason, and before/after records remain in the audit trail. A reversed journal must have its reversal removed before it can change; generated depreciation is rebuilt from the asset register rather than edited by hand.
- **Opening balances**: assigned editors and Super Admin enter balanced debit/credit amounts for balance-sheet accounts in **Akun**, for each academic year. They can change or clear them at any time, including after transactions exist. Each year's opening is managed independently; saving it preserves the journal count and replaces the full balance map.
- **Cash-flow classification**: `cashFlowAllocation` derives each journal's `cashFlows` from counter accounts' catalog sections. Hutang defaults to Operasional, Modal to Pendanaan, and 13xxx to Investasi. Legacy journals retain their recorded classification.
- **Depreciation**: straight-line, whole Rupiah, from the acquisition month. `POST_DEPRECIATION` creates or rebuilds the selected month's `depreciation-MM` journal from the current asset register. After changing or deleting an asset, repost depreciation in each affected month.
- **Bank reconciliation**: compare cash/bank balances with statement balances or cash counts, including deposits in transit and outstanding payments. Differences are informational and do not prevent editing or printing. Statement attachments remain private through `/api/satker-finance/receipt?reconciliationId=`.
- **Normal balances** drive Buku Besar and informational abnormal-balance warnings. Unit accounts can be marked contra. Used catalog accounts retain identity and accounting-target protections.
- **Negative-cash warning**: while a journal is typed, `findNegativeCash` (`negativeCash.ts`) warns when it would leave KAS or a bank account below zero on or after its date. Balances follow journal dates and are read at day end; editing replaces the journal being edited. It is a warning only; posting is never blocked.
- **Printable Arus Kas**: the monthly **Arus Kas** tab downloads `Lap Arus Kas - <initials> - Okt26.pdf`, followed by the month's attached transaction receipts. It uses the current books and requires no digital sign-off. Missing receipt downloads retain an explanatory page. Receipt access remains restricted to authorized users.
- **Printable Buku Besar**: the **Buku Besar** tab downloads `Buku Besar - <initials> - Nov26.pdf` for the selected month or the whole academic year, for one account or for every account with an opening balance or a line (`printableLedgers`, `generateSatkerLedgerPdf.ts`). Each account shows opening balance, lines with running balance, period totals and closing balance, in its normal direction. It carries no signatures; the letterhead is shared with Arus Kas in `satkerReportLayout.ts`.
- **Arus Kas signatures**: the monthly view places two signer cards beside the summary: **Ketua SatKer** and **WarKu**. Search active Loyalis, then adjust the printed name/title if needed. Current choices go straight to the PDF; **Simpan konfigurasi tanda tangan** saves them per SatKer for reuse across months and years. Unit editors and Super Admin save through `SAVE_CASH_FLOW_SIGNATURES` with the unit revision and a config audit record. The unit profile head and editor assignments remain independent. The API exposes only active Loyalis IDs, names, and job titles for this picker. Legacy units initially use their profile head and the unique active finance vice rector, without writing defaults.
- **Validation**: `npx tsx --test src/lib/satker-finance/*.test.ts`, `npm run test:satker-finance:page`, and `npm run test:satker-finance:integration` (Auth, Firestore, and Storage emulators; Java required).
